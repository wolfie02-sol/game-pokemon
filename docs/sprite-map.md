# Sprite map

Overworld characters are Claude Red's 16×24 cast (three walk frames each way). Creatures are that project's shape-rasterised Pokémon, drawn at 48×48 so battles, the summary and the Wildex keep their layout. Party and PC icons stay 16×16. The item on the ground is Claude Red's Poké Ball.

Tiles, the exclamation bubble, fonts and battle effects are unchanged. Claude Red has no exclamation-bubble sprite.

## Creatures

Each Wildkin keeps its name, types and stats. The sprite is the Kanto Pokémon whose base stats (and, where those were edited, whose family role) match. Battle art is rendered at 48×48, the size the battle, summary and Wildex screens already use. The 16×16 party icon is downscaled from that sprite. Back sprites are Claude Red's generated back view.

A few middle-dex lines had their stats rewritten, so a pure stat match paired them with the wrong family. Those seven are marked "family fix" and follow the evolution line instead.
